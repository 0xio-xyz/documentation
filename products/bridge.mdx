## Overview

The **0xio Bridge** connects the **Octra Network** with **Ethereum**, enabling bidirectional transfers between native OCT and wrapped OCT (wOCT, an ERC-20 token on Ethereum).

Unlike intent-based bridges, the 0xio Bridge uses a **light client architecture** — an Octra Light Client contract on Ethereum verifies Merkle proofs of Octra transactions, providing trustless verification without relying on external oracles.

## How It Works

### OCT → wOCT (Octra to Ethereum)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Wallets">
    Connect your 0xio wallet (Octra) and EVM wallet (MetaMask, Rabby, etc.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lock OCT">
    Lock OCT in the BridgeVault contract on Octra with your Ethereum destination address
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for Bridge Header">
    The bridge relayer submits epoch roots to the OctraLightClient on Ethereum (~2-5 minutes)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Claim wOCT">
    Once the epoch root is verified, claim your wOCT using a Merkle proof
  </Step>
</Steps>

### wOCT → OCT (Ethereum to Octra)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Approve wOCT">
    Approve the EthereumBridge contract to spend your wOCT
  </Step>
  <Step title="Burn wOCT">
    Burn wOCT on Ethereum with your Octra recipient address
  </Step>
  <Step title="Automatic Unlock">
    The bridge relayer detects the burn and unlocks OCT on Octra (~2 minutes)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Features

- **Trustless Verification**: Octra Light Client on Ethereum verifies Merkle proofs — no trusted oracle
- **Multi-Wallet Support**: EIP-6963 detection for Rabby, MetaMask, Phantom, and other EVM wallets
- **WalletConnect**: Scan QR code from the mobile app to bridge
- **Auto-Reconnect**: Wallets persist across page refreshes
- **Bridge History**: Full transaction history with claim actions and recovery
- **Network Dashboard**: Live chain stats at [bridge.0xio.xyz/octra](https://bridge.0xio.xyz/octra)
- **Bridge Stats**: Volume, transactions, unique wallets at [bridge.0xio.xyz/stats](https://bridge.0xio.xyz/stats)

## Bridge Limits

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Minimum lock | 1 OCT |
| Rate | 1 OCT = 1 wOCT (always) |
| Cooldown | 6 epochs per address between locks |
| wOCT → OCT | Automatic unlock via relayer (~2 min) |

## Architecture

```
User (0xio Wallet + EVM Wallet)
    │
    ├── Octra: BridgeVault (lock/unlock OCT)
    │
    ├── Ethereum: EthereumBridge + wOCT ERC-20
    │
    ├── OctraLightClient (Merkle proof verification)
    │
    └── Bridge Relayer (epoch root updater)
```

## Known Limitations

- **Epoch Sync Delay**: The ETH light client epoch updater may fall behind chain production. This causes Merkle proofs to be temporarily unverifiable until the sync catches up.
- **Pruned Epochs**: Old epoch roots are pruned from the light client contract. Bridge transactions referencing pruned epochs cannot be claimed — users should claim promptly.

## Security

- **No trusted oracle**: Verification is on-chain via Merkle proofs against the light client
- **Bridge pause**: Owner can pause the bridge during emergencies
- **Cooldown protection**: Rate limits prevent rapid drain attacks
- **Account change detection**: Frontend detects wallet account switches to prevent sending to wrong address

<Warning>
Always verify the full destination address in the confirmation dialog before bridging. The bridge shows the complete address (not truncated) to prevent address injection attacks.
</Warning>
