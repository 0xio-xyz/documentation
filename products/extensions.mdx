The **0xio Browser Extension** is a non-custodial wallet for the Octra Network with an EVM wallet built in. It keeps public and private (FHE-encrypted) OCT balances, holds a 0x address on Ethereum, Robinhood Chain and any EVM network you add, bridges between Octra and Ethereum, and connects to Octra and EVM websites.

<Note>
  **Status: Live (v3.0.2) on the Chrome Web Store.** Install from the [Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/0xio-wallet/anknhjilldkeelailocijnfibefmepcc) or from [0xio.xyz/install](https://0xio.xyz/install). v3.0.3 is in progress. The Firefox version is in review on Mozilla Add-ons.
</Note>

## Requirements

- Chromium browsers (Chrome, Brave, Edge, Opera) version 111 or later
- Firefox 140 or later (142 on Android)
- Manifest V3

## Getting Started

The welcome screen offers **Create a wallet** and **I already have a wallet**. The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are linked below them.

- **Create a wallet:** create a password, confirm it on the next screen, optionally add a passkey, choose your networks and land on **Your wallet is ready**, which lists your Octra and EVM addresses. You back up the recovery phrase afterwards, from the **Back up** tag on Home.
- **I already have a wallet:** paste a recovery phrase (12 or 24 words), a private key or an address into one field. The extension tells which it is, offers a one-tap fix for a mistyped word, and shows the wallet it found with its balance. An address adds a watch-only wallet.

The password needs at least 8 characters and unlocks the extension in this browser. See [Installation & Setup](/wallet/installation) for each step.

### Adding More Wallets

In the wallet list, **Add wallet** offers:

- **Create wallet:** a new wallet and recovery phrase, saved at once.
- **Add account:** another address from one of your wallets' recovery phrases. It gets its own Octra and 0x address.
- **Import wallet:** a phrase, a private key or an address (watch-only).

The wallet list shows where each wallet comes from (Phrase, Private key or Watch-only) and both of its addresses, each with a copy button. Switching wallets says which wallet it switched to.

- **Header:** your wallet's picture and name (click for the wallet list), the network switch (**All networks**, **Octra** or **EVM**) and Settings.
- **Balance:** the total in USD over a chart of the day (green up, red down, grey when flat). Tags at the top right of the balance card ask for action, most urgent first, for example **Back up** or **Set up private**.
- **Actions:** **Send**, then **Receive**, **Bridge**, **Privacy** and **History**. On EVM, Privacy stays visible but disabled.
- **Assets:** OCT, the encrypted (private) OCT row, Octra tokens and EVM assets, each with its USD value. Tap one for its page with a 1D, 7D or 1M price chart, the balance, actions and recent activity.
- **Status bar:** the open site's connection chip, a **0xio Desktop** chip when the desktop app is running private proofs, and the **Mainnet** or **Testnet** chip.

Balances that are not read yet show `--`, never 0. Home reads balances lightly every 30 seconds while open and not at all while hidden or locked.

See [Wallet Interface](/wallet/interface) for a full tour.

## Sending

- **Octra:** public OCT, Octra tokens and private sends. **Bulk** sends OCT to up to 5 recipients from the public balance.
- **EVM:** ETH and tokens on any EVM network that is on. See [EVM Networks](/wallet/evm#sending).
- **Fees:** one live network fee per send, read from the node. It drives the shown fee, **Max**, every balance check and the signature. You can pick another tier or a custom fee of 100 to 10,000,000 OU. If the fee cannot be read, Review is blocked with **Network fee unavailable**.
- **Review:** every send opens a review with **Back** and **Send**. After Send, a result screen shows **Sending**, then **Sent** once the network confirms it (**Queued** for private sends).

See [Sending Transactions](/wallet/sending).

## Privacy

The **Privacy** screen moves OCT between your public and private balances and claims private transfers sent to you:

- **Encrypt** (public to private), **Decrypt** (private to public) and **Claim**, each with a review and the live network fee.
- **Set up private:** a wallet with no encryption key on chain shows a **Set up private** tag on Home. Its sheet shows the one-time network fee, and **Turn on** registers the key.
- **Private on hold:** when the network holds a private balance (for example an older format waiting for a migration), Home shows a tag and the Encrypted row reads **On hold**. The sheet shows the node's reason.

All proofs run on your device. With [0xio Desktop](/products/desktop) running, they run in the desktop app instead, which is faster. See [Privacy Operations](/wallet/privacy-operations).

## EVM Wallet

Every wallet made from a recovery phrase also has a 0x address. The extension shows ETH and tokens, sends, receives, shows EVM NFTs and lists EVM history next to Octra. Networks can be turned on or off, given your own RPC, or added from a list of popular chains or the public chain list. See [EVM Networks](/wallet/evm).

## Bridge

**Bridge** on Home bridges between Octra and Ethereum without leaving the wallet:

- **Bridge to Ethereum:** OCT on Octra becomes wOCT on Ethereum, claimed with the wallet's 0x address.
- **Bridge to Octra:** wOCT on Ethereum becomes OCT on Octra.
- **Any chain to OCT:** pay with a token on another chain and receive OCT on Octra.
- **Claim:** a page for transfers to Ethereum, grouped as Ready, In progress and Completed, with **Find my transfers** and **Add by hash**.

Each step has its own review, and the Octra network fee is read live. See [0xio Bridge](/products/bridge#bridge-inside-the-extension).

## History

History lists Octra and EVM activity together, newest first, on two pages: **Mainnet** and **Testnet**. Contract calls read as actions (Swap, Approve, Bridge or the method in words) with the contract named. Every row opens a details card, and contract calls have a **View call data** page. See [Wallet Interface](/wallet/interface#history).

## NFTs

- Every collection the wallet holds shows without an import step; **Import** stays for other contracts.
- The three newest pieces come first, then one row per collection, then **Hidden**.
- Hide a piece or a whole collection with Undo. Hidden items are kept per network and wallet.
- A piece's page shows its art, traits, status, holder and contract, with **List for sale** and **Send**, or **Change price** and **Cancel listing** for a listed piece. Every step opens a review with the fee.
- Octra collections (SNS-1, Bionts and living NFTs) and EVM collections (ERC-721 and ERC-1155) share the list. See [NFT Standards](/wallet/nft-standards).

## Connecting to Sites

The extension connects to Octra sites through the 0xio SDK and to EVM sites, which list it as **0xio Wallet**. EVM sites can be connected to several of your wallets, and a site only ever sees the wallet in use. The status bar shows the open site's connection, and **Settings > Connections > Connected sites** lists them all. See [Connecting to Sites](/wallet/connecting-sites).

## Settings

Settings opens on your wallet, then:

- **Security:** password, passkey unlock, auto-lock time, and Reset wallet under This browser.
- **Backup:** recovery phrase and private key (the EVM key too while EVM is on). Shows **Back up again** when a wallet needs a new backup.
- **Connections:** connected sites and the address book.
- **Appearance:** theme (Dim, Day or Octra) and language (English, Chinese, Japanese, Korean or Indonesian).
- **Networks:** Octra (Mainnet, Devnet or a node you add, and the optional RPC proxy), then the EVM networks.
- **Tokens and NFTs:** a switch for every token and collection, shown or hidden as likely spam. Imported tokens carry an **Imported** tag.
- **EVM:** turn EVM on or off for the open wallet.

**Lock wallet** sits at the bottom, with links to these docs, the Terms and the [Privacy Policy](https://0xio.xyz/privacy).

## Locking

- The toolbar icon shows a padlock while the wallet is locked.
- Unlock with your password, or tap **Use passkey** where a passkey is set up. The passkey prompt never opens by itself.
- Locking shuts down the private balance engine and clears decrypted private balances and wallet data held in memory.

## Notices

Messages appear as one small pill above the status bar. Information fades after 4 seconds, errors and warnings after 6 seconds. Hovering pauses it. Copy buttons show **Copied** in place, and successful actions show in the screen itself rather than as a notice.

## RFC-O-1 Provider

The extension implements the **RFC-O-1** wallet provider standard and exposes `window.octra`:

```javascript
const accounts = await window.octra.request({ method: 'octra_requestAccounts' });
const balance = await window.octra.request({ method: 'octra_getEncryptedBalance' });

window.octra.on('accountsChanged', (accounts) => { /* ... */ });
window.octra.on('connect', (info) => { /* ... */ });
```

The `@0xio/sdk` includes an `OctraProviderAdapter` that uses `window.octra` as a transport. For EVM sites the extension is a standard EIP-1193 provider, see [EVM Provider](/developers/sdk-guide#evm-provider).

## Desktop Companion

When [0xio Desktop](/products/desktop) runs on the same computer, the extension connects to it and runs private proofs there, which is faster than in the browser.

- **Automatic:** no setup. The extension finds the desktop app on `127.0.0.1`.
- **Status:** the status bar shows a **0xio Desktop** chip, which tells whether proofs run in the desktop app or in this browser.
- **No signing key:** the desktop app builds proofs but never receives the key that signs your transactions.
