## Overview

**0xio DEX** is an exchange on Octra. Swaps go through on-chain pools that hold liquidity in price bins (the Liquidity Book design, also known as DLMM), and anyone can add liquidity to a pool over a price range. It has two main tabs, **Swap** and **Pools**, plus **Activity** and **Stats**, and works only with the 0xio wallet.

The earlier cross-chain swap (OCT to ETH through a solver) is retired: the DEX removed it on 2026-11-22 and the extension in v3.0.7.

## How a Pool Works

- **Bins:** each pool pairs two tokens and splits the price into small fixed steps called bins. Only one bin, the active bin, holds both tokens. Bins above it hold only the first token, bins below it only the second.
- **Price:** a swap trades at the active bin's fixed price until that bin runs out, then moves to the next bin, up to 50 bins in one swap.
- **OCT:** pools hold Wrapped OCT (WOCT), one to one with OCT. The DEX wraps and unwraps for you as steps of the same swap.

## Swap

Pick what you pay and what you receive. The swap card shows the pool price, the price impact, the live fee and the minimum you receive after slippage (0.5, 1 or 2%).

Every swap is a short plan of steps, all listed in the review before anything is signed:

| Swap | Steps |
|------|-------|
| OCT to a token | Wrap OCT, approve WOCT, swap |
| A token to OCT | Approve, swap, unwrap exactly the WOCT the swap returned |
| Between two pool tokens | Approve, swap |

An approval is skipped when the allowance already covers the amount. The wallet asks for each step, and the next step waits until the previous one is confirmed on chain. A step that is not confirmed in time is shown as not confirmed and is never sent again. If a swap fails after the wrap, the DEX offers to unwrap the WOCT left in your wallet.

## Pools

A pool opens on its bin chart, which you can pan and zoom.

- **Add:** type an amount and the deposit is spread over a range centred on the current price. **Adjust range** offers **Narrow**, **Balanced** (the default) or **Wide**, and a shape: **Spot** (even), **Curve** (more near the price) or **Bid-ask** (more at the edges). Bins below the price receive one token and bins above it the other; the active bin takes the mix it already holds.
- **Remove:** take out a share of your bins.
- **Move range:** a position the price has left can be moved back around the price; this removes the old bins and adds the same width around the price.
- **Positions:** each shows whether it is in range or out of range.

One add or remove covers up to 20 bins per transaction. If the active bin moves between your review and confirming, nothing is sent and the form follows the new price for the next review.

## Fees

- **Dynamic fee:** every swap pays a base fee plus a part that rises with recent price movement and fades again when the market is calm. The fee is capped at 10%. The fee shown before you swap is the live rate the pool reports.
- **Where it goes:** most of the fee stays in the bins for the liquidity providers there; the protocol keeps a share (10% by default).
- **Composition fee:** adding to the active bin with a mix that differs from what the bin holds swaps part of your deposit inside the bin. That part pays the same rate as a swap. The review shows this fee before you sign.

## Tokens and Pools

Tokens and pools come from the 0xio token list on GitHub. Each entry is checked on chain before use, and the last good copy is kept for when the list or the node cannot be reached. Nothing is built into the app.
