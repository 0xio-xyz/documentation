The 0xio wallet ships as a browser extension, a desktop app and a mobile app. All three use the same key derivation, so a recovery phrase opens the same Octra wallet in every client. This guide focuses on the browser extension; for desktop and mobile, see [Desktop Wallet](/products/desktop) and [Mobile Wallet](/products/mobile).

## System Requirements

### Supported Browsers

The extension is a Manifest V3 build and runs on Chromium browsers and Firefox.

<CardGroup cols="2">
  <Card title="Chrome" icon="chrome">
    Version 111 or later
  </Card>
  <Card title="Brave, Edge and Opera" icon="globe">
    Any version built on Chromium 111 or later
  </Card>
  <Card title="Firefox" icon="firefox">
    Version 140 or later (142 on Android). In review on Mozilla Add-ons
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

### Current Version

**Extension v3.0.2**, published on the Chrome Web Store. v3.0.3 is in progress.

<Note>
Other clients version independently: **Desktop v0.5.5** and **Mobile v1.3.3**. Every client installs from [0xio.xyz/install](https://0xio.xyz/install).
</Note>

## Installation Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Chrome Web Store listing">
    Go to the [0xio Wallet listing](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/0xio-wallet/anknhjilldkeelailocijnfibefmepcc) on the Chrome Web Store.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Extension">
    Click "Add to Chrome" (the button adapts to Brave, Edge and Opera) and confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pin to Toolbar">
    Pin the extension to your browser toolbar for easy access. The icon shows a padlock while the wallet is locked.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Launch Setup">
    Click the 0xio extension icon to begin setup.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Creating Your First Wallet

The welcome screen offers two ways in: **Create a wallet** and **I already have a wallet**. The Terms of Service and Privacy Policy are linked below them.

<Frame caption="The welcome screen">
  <img src="/images/extension/welcome.png" alt="0xio extension welcome screen with Create a wallet and I already have a wallet" width="320" />
</Frame>


<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a password">
    Choose a password of at least 8 characters. It unlocks 0xio in this browser and never leaves this device. Confirm it on the next screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Passkey (optional)">
    Where the browser supports it, you are offered to unlock with a passkey (Touch ID, Windows Hello or a security key). **Not now** skips it; you can add one later in Settings > Security. Passkeys are not available on Firefox.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your networks">
    Octra is always on. EVM is on by default and gives the wallet a 0x address from the same recovery phrase. See [EVM Networks](/wallet/evm).

    <Frame caption="Choose your networks">
      <img src="/images/extension/choose-networks.png" alt="Choose your networks step with Octra always on and an EVM switch" width="280" />
    </Frame>

  </Step>
  <Step title="Your wallet is ready">
    The last screen lists your Octra address and, with EVM on, your 0x address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
Your password derives a key with PBKDF2-SHA256 (900,000 iterations on the extension) that protects your vault with AES-256-GCM. It only locks the wallet on this device. Your recovery phrase is what controls your funds on every device.
</Info>

### Back Up Your Recovery Phrase

A new wallet is usable at once; you back up its recovery phrase from Home. Until you do, Home shows a **Back up** tag and Receive asks you to back up first.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the backup">
    Tap the **Back up** tag on Home and enter your password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Show the words">
    The 12 words stay blurred until you hold **Hold to show**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write them down">
    Write the words down in order, offline, then tap **I have written it down**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
**CRITICAL SECURITY INFORMATION**

Your 12-word Recovery Phrase is the master key to your funds. 0xio generates this phrase **locally on your device** using the BIP-39 standard with cryptographic randomness. The Octra master key is derived as `HMAC-SHA512("Octra seed", seed)[0:32]` (no BIP-44 path), and your Octra address is `SHA-256(public key)` encoded as Base58 with an `oct` prefix. The EVM address comes from the same phrase at `m/44'/60'/0'/0/0`.

**If you lose this phrase, your funds are permanently inaccessible.**

0xio cannot recover lost phrases as they never leave your device.
</Warning>

#### Best Practices - DO:

- Write it down on paper immediately
- Store in a fireproof safe
- Consider metal backup plates for long-term storage
- Split across multiple secure locations for large holdings
- Test your backup by importing on a second device

#### Best Practices - DON'T:

- Store in cloud services (Google Drive, Dropbox, iCloud and similar)
- Take photos of your phrase
- Store in email or messaging apps
- Store digitally on internet-connected devices
- Share with anyone, including support staff

## Importing an Existing Wallet

If you already have a 0xio wallet or another Octra wallet:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Import">
    Click **I already have a wallet** on the welcome screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste your phrase or key">
    Paste or type a 12 or 24 word recovery phrase, or an Octra private key, into the one field. The extension tells which it is and offers a one-tap fix for a mistyped word.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the wallet it found">
    Before you continue, the wallet shows the address it found with its balance. Check it is the one you expect.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Password and networks">
    Create a password for this device and choose your networks, as above.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
A wallet imported from an Octra private key has no EVM address; only a recovery phrase gives both. An EVM private key or a 0x address pasted into the field is named as such and not imported as an Octra wallet.
</Note>

## Watch-Only Wallets

Follow any Octra address without its keys: paste the `oct...` address into the same import field. Its balance shows before you add it.

**Use Cases:**
- Monitoring cold wallet balances
- Observing project treasury addresses
- Keeping an eye on another device's wallet

<Note>
A watch-only wallet can view balances and history but cannot send, sign, bridge or run privacy operations.
</Note>
