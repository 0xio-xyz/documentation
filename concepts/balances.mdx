Every 0xio wallet holds two distinct balance types on the same Octra address, giving you full control over your financial privacy.

- **Public balance (OCT):** a normal, transparent on-chain balance.
- **Private balance (pOCT):** an FHE-encrypted balance whose amount is hidden from everyone except you.

## Public Balance

Your **Public Balance** (OCT) is visible to everyone on the blockchain.

### Characteristics

- **Transparency:** Visible to anyone viewing the blockchain
- **Speed:** Faster transaction processing — no proof generation required
- **Compatibility:** Works with all protocols and services

### When to Use

- Standard interactions with public DeFi protocols
- Sending to exchanges that don't support FHE
- When transparency is beneficial or required
- Situations where speed and lower cost are priorities

## Private Balance

Your **Private Balance** (pOCT) is encrypted with Octra's FHE layer and only visible to you.

### Characteristics

- **Privacy:** Amount encrypted on-chain; completely confidential
- **Security:** Complete financial privacy
- **Validation:** Transfers are validated with zero-knowledge range proofs without revealing amounts
- **Cost:** Privacy operations carry a larger fee because the network must process ciphertexts and verify proofs

### When to Use

- Confidential transfers and payments
- Private trading and swaps
- Salary payments and business transactions
- Protecting wealth information from public view

<Info>
Your 0xio wallet displays both balances simultaneously. You move funds between them at any time using **Encrypt** (public → private) and **Decrypt** (private → public), and receive incoming private transfers with **Claim**.
</Info>

## Moving Between Balances

### Encrypting Funds (Public → Private)

This "shields" your funds — moving OCT into your encrypted pOCT balance so the amount is no longer visible to blockchain observers.

**Process:**
1. Open the Encrypt action
2. Enter the amount to shield
3. Review the fee
4. Confirm the transaction
5. Wait for blockchain confirmation

**Technical note:** Your device generates the FHE ciphertext and the supporting range proof locally before broadcasting. Proof generation can take anywhere from seconds (native desktop) to over a minute (mobile), depending on platform.

### Decrypting Funds (Private → Public)

This "unshields" funds — moving pOCT back into your public OCT balance, making the amount visible on the blockchain again. A zero proof attests that the remaining encrypted balance stays consistent.

**Process:**
1. Open the Decrypt action
2. Enter the amount to unshield
3. Review the fee
4. Confirm the transaction

### Claiming Incoming Private Transfers

When someone sends you a private (stealth) transfer, the funds arrive as a pending encrypted output rather than landing in your balance automatically. **Claim** scans incoming outputs for those addressed to you and adds them to your private balance.

## Network Fees

Octra denominates fees in **ou**, the network's base fee unit (1 OCT = 1,000,000 ou).

- Standard public transfers carry a small, flat fee.
- Stealth and other privacy operations require a minimum fee of **1,000,000 ou (1 OCT)** because the network must process ciphertexts and verify zero-knowledge proofs.

Exact fees are estimated by the wallet at the time of each transaction.
