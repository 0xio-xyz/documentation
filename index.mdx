## What is 0xio?

**0xio** is a set of non-custodial wallets and tools for the **Octra Network**. It makes Octra's encrypted balances and private transfers usable from a browser, a desktop and a phone.

Octra provides the Layer-1 blockchain and its encryption. 0xio provides the wallets, SDKs and applications that let users and developers work with it. The browser extension also holds an EVM wallet for Ethereum and other EVM networks, and the 0xio Bridge moves OCT between Octra and Ethereum.

## Core Features

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Privacy by Default" icon="lock">
    0xio integrates Octra's **Hypergraph Fully Homomorphic Encryption (HFHE)** so balances, transfers, and application logic can remain encrypted throughout their lifecycle. Validators process encrypted payloads without learning their contents.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Non-Custodial Security" icon="key">
    All keys are generated and stored locally. 0xio cannot freeze, access, or recover user funds. You maintain complete sovereignty over your assets with industry-standard cryptography (BIP-39 mnemonics, Ed25519 signing, AES-256-GCM vault encryption, PBKDF2).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Client-Side Encryption" icon="microchip">
    Sensitive data is encrypted **before** it is broadcast to the network. All FHE operations happen on your device, ensuring zero trust in intermediaries.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Dual Balance System" icon="scale-balanced">
    Hold both Public and Private balances. Choose transparency for lower costs or privacy for confidential transactions, and move between them with encrypt and decrypt.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Octra and EVM in One Wallet" icon="layer-group">
    In the browser extension, one recovery phrase gives each wallet an Octra address and a 0x address. Balances, sends, NFTs and history for Ethereum, Robinhood Chain and any EVM network you add sit next to Octra.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A Review Before Every Signature" icon="eye">
    Every transaction, approval and signature opens a review with the signing wallet, the network and the live fee before anything is signed.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Why Privacy Matters

On most blockchains:
- Balances are public
- Transactions are globally visible
- Activity can be trivially tracked
- Financial privacy doesn't exist

With 0xio on Octra:
- Private balances when you want them
- Encrypted transactions without revealing amounts
- Applications that operate on private state
- Privacy enforced by cryptography, not trust
